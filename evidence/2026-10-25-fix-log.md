## Fix 8 in detail, because it changes what "green tests" meant

The daemon died in production with:

```
fatal error: concurrent map read and map write
  attribution.(*Attributor).groupFor
  attribution.(*Attributor).Observe
  pipeline.(*Pipeline).ObserveProcesses
```

Two goroutines drive the Attributor on different cadences (process sampler
15 s, system sampler 5 s). Nothing had exercised both at once until the
pipeline was wired end to end. The whole suite was green while the daemon
crashed.

`make ci` now runs `go test -race`. The plain target cannot see this class of
bug, and a green suite that ships a crashing daemon is worse than a red one.
